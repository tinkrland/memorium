# prixie

prixie is a personal presence proxy. she joins meetings you cant or dont want to attend, listens for the specific things you asked her to find, asks your pre approved questions if the floor opens, and comes back with exactly what you needed — not a summary, but the actual answers, codes, links, and decisions you sent her for.

prixie is the pilot application of the memorium architecture — memorium is her memory, and every meeting she attends is ingested through memorium's ingestion and retrieval pipeline.
