# about

prixie is a personal presence proxy. she joins meetings you cant or dont want to attend, listens for the specific things you asked her to find, asks your pre approved questions if the floor opens, and comes back with exactly what you needed. not a summary. not a recap. the actual answers, codes, links, and decisions you sent her for.

most meeting tools (granola, otter, fireflies, tl;dv) sit next to you and take notes while you talk. prixie goes instead of you. you are asleep, at another meeting, on a different timezone, or just not interested in spending an hour for one fact. prixie handles the showing up part and brings back only what matters to you.

the core idea is simple: you should not have to be somewhere to get what you need from it.

prixie is lowercase, personal, and not a product pitch. she is your stand in.
