# the problem

you cant be everywhere. but you need things from places you cant be.

a hackathon kickoff call at 3am your time. a webinar that overlaps with your actual work. a community office hours session where the only thing you need is one link they will paste in chat. a quarterly review where you need to ask one question during q&a and the answer determines your roadmap.

the default solution is to either skip it (and miss whatever you needed) or attend it (and lose an hour for one fact).

meeting tools like granola, otter, fireflies, and tl;dv do not solve this. they assume you are in the meeting. they take notes for you. they transcribe what you already heard. they are companions, not proxies.

prixie is a proxy. she goes instead of you. she does not take notes about what you experienced. she experiences it for you and brings back only what you asked for.

the difference is not subtle. a note taker helps you remember what happened in a meeting you attended. prixie attends a meeting you did not go to and tells you the specific thing you needed from it.

no generic summaries. no "here is what was discussed." just: you asked about the API timeline. they said q1. you asked for the access code. it is PRIXIE BUILD 2026 X9. you asked for the submission form. it is in the captured items. done.

that is the problem. that is what prixie solves. everything else is implementation.
